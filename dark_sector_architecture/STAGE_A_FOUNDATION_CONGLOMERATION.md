Those results do not establish the dark-sector hypothesis. Green-Wald weak-limit results, relativistic simulation results showing strong gauge dependence of inferred backreaction, and exact inhomogeneous models that fail late-time growth suppression remain mandatory adversarial constraints.

The early-Universe constraint is equally important. Planck separately constrains the baryon budget and the gravitational behavior represented in standard LambdaCDM by a much larger cold-dark-matter-like density. Therefore a complete no-new-dark-substance architecture cannot begin only after late nonlinear structure formation. It must reproduce the CMB acoustic structure, early gravitational potentials, BAO/BBN consistency, and lensing while respecting the baryon density.
