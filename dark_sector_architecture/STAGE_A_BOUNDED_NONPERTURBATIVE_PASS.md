## Frozen question

Does existing literature contain a standard-GR, non-material, non-force, nonperturbative or additional architecture that supplies the persistent pre-recombination gravitational function normally assigned to cold dark matter and continues coherently into the late structure/acceleration architecture?

## Candidate families examined

### Nonlocal / memory gravity

Nonlocal gravity can mathematically simulate effective dark matter through causal kernels and memory terms. These constructions are informative because they demonstrate the kind of history-dependent operator capable of creating an effective source.

They do not satisfy the frozen native-model gate because the nonlocal kernel or memory action is an added modification of the Einstein dynamics.

### Pure-GR gravitational-wave self-organization

Brill-Hartle / Anderson-Brill geons establish that nonlinear gravitational-wave self-gravity can form self-consistent geometric configurations in approximation schemes.

The route does not qualify as the required cosmological architecture:
- high-frequency GW stress energy is radiation/null-like rather than pressureless;
- long-lived stability of asymptotically flat pure-GR geons is not established and has adverse results;
- no cosmological abundance/transfer calculation reproduces the recombination CMB burden;
- a population of compact geons would function as an inventory of gravitating objects rather than the system-level architecture hypothesized here.

### Edge / boundary architecture

Umeh 2026 identifies gravitational edge modes generated by spacetime surgery at matter horizons in a covariant GR construction and shows a flat-rotation-curve application without particle dark matter.

This is a strong **partial-architecture precedent** because the additional gravity is generated by system boundaries and multiscale organization rather than a new particle.

It is not a full-route solution because the construction is tied to nonlinear structure/caustic formation and has not been demonstrated before recombination or against the CMB acoustic/lensing burden.

### GR field self-interaction / gravito-magnetic proposals

Deur's GR self-interaction model and Le Corre's gravitic-field program are relevant because they attempt to obtain DM-like and DE-like functions without exotic matter or a modified force law.

Their current literature status does not close the early-Universe requirement:
- Deur models the main structural transition after large structures form, takes the early depletion factor near unity, and explicitly leaves detailed CMB/BAO predictions beyond scope.
- Le Corre states that the required large gravitic-field value needs an additional/ad hoc hypothesis, that persistence and magnitude in full nonlinear GR remain open, and that deeper cosmological simulations are still required.

### Exact inhomogeneous GR

LTB/Szekeres and related exact solutions show that inhomogeneity can materially change distance and curvature inference.

They do not presently replace the pre-recombination pressureless gravitational mode and commonly retain dust/matter assumptions rather than deriving the missing function from geometry alone.
