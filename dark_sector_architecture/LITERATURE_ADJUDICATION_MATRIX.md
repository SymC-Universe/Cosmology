## Controlled synthesis after Batch 001

Admitted foundations currently support:
- ontology neutrality of the conventional DM/DE decomposition;
- native cosmological modal organization and scale/time-dependent transfer;
- averaged-GR structure/curvature coupling;
- several forms of two-way multiscale coupling;
- formal history dependence;
- limited late-time DM-like and DE-like functional analogies.

Admitted counterevidence currently establishes that:
- known radiation-era/linear backreaction terms are too small or have the wrong scaling to supply the standard pre-recombination pressureless gravitational function;
- large backreaction claims are sensitive to theorem assumptions, gauge/slicing, and closure;
- apparent acceleration alone is insufficient if growth suppression fails.

The controlled literature lane therefore remains open on:
- primordial modal/conglomerate inheritance;
- non-substance realization of a persistent non-acoustic gravitational mode;
- full scalar/modal/conglomerate joint meaning;
- cross-epoch closure between primordial organization and late averaged-GR architecture.

No current refusal closes P0-D exploration.
