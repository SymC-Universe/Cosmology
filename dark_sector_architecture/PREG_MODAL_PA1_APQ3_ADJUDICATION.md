### A2. Algebraic/reduction attack

**Class:** MATERIAL, resolved for representation independence.

Known theory aligns E and sigma in linear growing-mode and restrictive silent/local models, so nonzero relation is not guaranteed and alignment is an explicit negative-control limit.

However, density/expansion and the separate spectra do not algebraically determine the relative eigenframe. The synthetic MR-02 known truth constructs identical separate spectra/invariants with different relative frames and a different commutator.

**Plan Delta:** B0 must contain the separate tensor spectra/invariants. B1 may add only relational geometry.

### A3. Newtonian-equivalence attack

**Class:** MATERIAL, unresolved as mechanism but resolved as claim scope.

Electric Weyl approaches the Newtonian tidal tensor in the weak-field limit, and nonlinear tidal evolution is already nonlocal in Newtonian gravity. A positive result cannot be described as a newly discovered relativistic force or uniquely relativistic effect.

**Plan Delta:** PREG-MODAL-PA-1 becomes an information/organization claim within native cosmological dynamics. Standard Newtonian tidal/velocity-shear predictors are part of the strongest null/comparator. Relativistic-mechanism language requires a separately qualified difference beyond that comparator.

### A4. Observer/gauge attack

**Class:** MATERIAL, carried as a representation limit.

The qualified first-order electric Weyl tensor is defined relative to the FLRW normal observer in Poisson gauge. At first perturbative order the background Weyl vanishes, so a first-order velocity change does not alter the first-order E object; this does not license nonlinear observer independence.

EBWeyl literature confirms that electric/magnetic Weyl splitting is observer dependent outside such controlled regimes.

**Plan Delta:** the primary P1-M claim, if activated, is explicitly limited to the frozen gevolution weak-field Poisson-gauge representation. Cross-observer/covariant or full-GR mechanism language requires a later robustness gate using invariant/covariant quantities or a second implementation.

### A5. Shell-crossing / velocity-field semantics

**Class:** MATERIAL, requires P0-Q qualification before P1 freeze.

After shell crossing, collisionless matter is multistream and the mass-weighted grid velocity is not a single dust 4-velocity. Vorticity and velocity dispersion become physical and resolution dependent.

**Plan Delta:** call the gevolution object `coarse-grained peculiar-velocity shear`, not exact dust shear, whenever multistreaming may matter. P0-Q must measure vorticity/dispersion or an equivalent velocity-field validity diagnostic and freeze an admissible scale/redshift domain before P1.

### A6. Material-lineage attack

**Class:** MATERIAL, resolved mechanically.

**Plan Delta:** material patches will be defined prospectively at t0 from particle IDs and followed by those same IDs. Fixed Eulerian-cell matching is prohibited as the primary lineage test.

### A7. Trivial-persistence attack

**Class:** MATERIAL, resolved by changing the endpoint.

Predicting the later E-sigma relation from the current relation could win through simple persistence.

**Plan Delta:** the primary endpoint is later **change in the separate modal spectra**, not the later relational state. B0 includes the current separate spectra; B1 adds the current relational geometry. The test asks whether the relation predicts how the components subsequently change.

**Plan Delta:** P0-Q must define non-overlapping material patches and blocked spatial validation. P1 evidence must treat independently seeded volumes as the primary replication unit; patch counts cannot be reported as independent N.
