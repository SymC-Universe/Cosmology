# Stage A Primordial Carrier Conglomeration

**Stage:** P0-D foundation synthesis  
**Question:** Does standard cosmology already contain a primordial architecture carrier that can transmit organized information across cosmic epochs without being a new substance?

## Result

Yes, in a limited but important sense.

The gauge-invariant curvature perturbation, commonly represented by (zeta) on uniform-density hypersurfaces or (mathcal R) on comoving hypersurfaces, is a well-established primordial information carrier.

For adiabatic perturbations on sufficiently large scales, it is conserved under standard conditions. Weinberg's adiabatic-mode theorem further establishes a large-wavelength adiabatic solution with nonzero constant (mathcal R) through arbitrary cosmic eras under mild assumptions.

This gives the cosmology investigation a literature-native inheritance carrier.

It does **not** give the system an independent dark-matter-like gravitational source.

## Why carrier is not source

Before recombination, the baryon-photon sector is a pressure-supported acoustic system. Its perturbations oscillate and experience diffusion damping. In standard cosmology, a pressureless component supplies the non-oscillating gravitational potential wells that continue to seed structure and shape the acoustic peak pattern.

Therefore

The first is an initial-information/inheritance statement. The second is a dynamical-source requirement.

## Standard-GR constraint

Linear scalar metric perturbations are related to the matter/radiation variables by the Einstein constraint equations. The scalar geometry is therefore not a freely specifiable independent reservoir once the physical stress-energy and initial conditions are fixed.

This is the central obstacle for a full no-dark-substance primordial route within ordinary linear GR.

## Residual

The next Stage A question is not "what carries inheritance?"

It is:

> Can native GR provide a non-substance pre-recombination gravitational source, generated by nonlinear geometry, correlations, Weyl/tidal structure, or coarse-grained dynamics, that converts the inherited primordial organization into the persistent scalar gravitational response required by the CMB?

Candidate classes to test:
- second-order/nonlinear scalar mode coupling;
- Weyl/tidal scalar organization;
- covariant correlation tensors;
- primordial geometrical correlations;
- nonlocal/coarse-grained gravitational response.

Each candidate must be classified as:
1. independent physical degree of freedom;
2. constrained metric response;
3. averaging/correlation term;
4. effective rewriting;
5. genuine source.

## Claim ceiling

**Established from literature:** primordial information inheritance through conserved curvature modes.

**Not established:** a primordial non-substance source sufficient to replace the cold-dark-matter gravitational function.
