# Partial-Architecture Residual Preregistration Candidates

**Project:** Cosmic Stability Architecture  
**Status:** P0-Q candidate construction only; no PREG-S/M/C/J claim activated  
**Parent literature freeze:** PREG-LIT-COSMO-v1.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-27

## Purpose

Translate the controlled Stage A literature residual into candidate preregistration records without converting synthesis-derived relationships into confirmation.

The active branch is the partial architecture:

> Determine which late-time phenomena conventionally assigned to dark matter and dark energy are genuinely generated, reorganized, or conditioned by GR-native multiscale structure, geometry, modal organization, feedback, edge/boundary effects, and inheritance, while leaving the independently required early gravitational component unspecified.

The full no-dark-substance route remains P0-D only and is not mixed into these candidate claims.

## Candidate-construction rules

1. Literature-admitted facts may define the native objects and fair comparators, but cannot serve as untouched confirmation.
2. A candidate is not activated until all MFR-14 fields are frozen and a decisive evidence route remains untouched.
3. No cosmological lower-case chi is introduced unless it separately satisfies the GOM licensing rule.
4. A system claim does not inherit support from scalar or modal success.
5. Distance-only apparent acceleration is insufficient; growth must be tested jointly where the claim concerns late acceleration.
6. Gauge/slicing robustness is mandatory for any backreaction or multiscale claim.
7. The partial branch may include standard CDM and Lambda in the comparator. It does not require their removal.

## PREG-S candidate lane

### PREG-S-CAND-00 — NO ACTIVATION CANDIDATE YET

**Disposition:** HOLD / NO_MATERIAL_RESIDUAL_SCALAR_CLAIM

### Rationale

The controlled literature currently supplies scalar objects and scalar markers, including curvature variables, averaged scalars, and the previously published model-conditional cosmological balance coordinate. It does not yet leave an irreducible late-time scalar claim that is both:

- genuinely residual after prior literature;
- independently predictive;
- native to the partial architecture;
- and not merely a projection of a modal or conglomerate result.

The strongest late-time residuals are presently modal, conglomerate, and joint.

### Activation criterion

A PREG-S claim may be created only if a native scalar observable or scalar coordinate is shown prospectively to add predictive or discriminative value beyond the strongest native baseline and beyond the system/modal representation from which it is derived.

### Failure consequence

No scalar claim is required for the partial architecture to proceed. Failure to identify one returns `NO_SCALAR_CLAIM_REQUIRED`, not failure of PREG-M/C/J.

## PREG-M candidate lane

### PREG-M-CAND-01 — Late-time modal reorganization carries observable information beyond a homogeneous-background baseline

**Candidate claim text**

For a fixed native GR cosmological model and explicitly defined late-time scale/epoch domain, the dynamical state exhibits scale-dependent modal/subspace reorganization associated with nonlinear structure formation, and at least one preregistered modal quantity provides reproducible predictive information for a late-time observable beyond the strongest matched homogeneous or linearized native comparator.

**Scope**

Late matter era through nonlinear structure formation. No claim about replacing the pre-recombination pressureless mode.

**MFR-02 provenance**

DOMAIN_THEORY + FRAMEWORK_DERIVED.

**MFR-03 target native object**

A preregistered spectrum/subspace/participation object derived from a native GR evolution operator, Jacobian, transfer matrix, relativistic Lagrangian deformation operator, or other explicitly defined dynamical representation.

**MFR-04 representation / validity regime**

Must state:
- exact gauge/covariant construction;
- scale range;
- epoch range;
- species content;
- linear/nonlinear regime;
- whether CDM and Lambda are retained;
- dimensional reduction, if any.

Refuse if the modal object changes qualitatively under admissible gauge/slicing choices or if it is only a numerical decomposition with no stable physical interpretation.

**MFR-05 strongest native comparator**

Matched standard LambdaCDM linear Einstein-Boltzmann evolution and, where nonlinear structure is used, the strongest available matched standard GR N-body/relativistic baseline using the same background parameters and initial conditions.

**MFR-06 competing explanation**

The apparent modal signal is only a generic consequence of nonlinear structure formation already captured by the native comparator and carries no additional architecture-specific information.

**MFR-07 expected response**

A preregistered modal quantity changes systematically with nonlinear structure/scale and predicts a held-out late-time response better than the frozen baseline, without retuning modal definitions after seeing outcomes.

**MFR-08 decision rule candidate**

Promotion requires all of:
1. representation stability under the frozen admissible gauge/slicing checks;
2. reproducible modal quantity under rerun/resolution checks;
3. preregistered directional or rank-order prediction on untouched data/simulations;
4. measurable added value over the strongest native comparator.

Otherwise:
- `NO_ADDED_MODAL_VALUE`, or
- `REPRESENTATION_UNSTABLE`, or
- `INDETERMINATE`.

**MFR-09 uncertainty / indeterminate zone**

Must be set from numerical convergence, finite-volume/finite-resolution variation, mode-identification uncertainty, and gauge/representation sensitivity before the decisive holdout is opened.

**MFR-10 independence / leakage map**

Training/discovery simulations, literature-derived thresholds, basis definitions, and parameter choices must be separated from the decisive test realization(s).

**MFR-11 multiplicity**

Freeze the candidate mode family, scale windows, epochs, observables, and allowed representation transforms.

**MFR-12 decisive evidence route candidate**

Independent relativistic simulation realization(s) or a withheld simulation volume/epoch not used to define the modal basis or select the target statistic.

**MFR-13 explicit falsifier**

The preregistered modal quantity is not stable under admissible representation checks, does not reproduce, or adds no predictive/discriminative value beyond the frozen native comparator on untouched evidence.

**MFR-14 failure consequence**

`NO_ADDED_MODAL_VALUE` or `DOMAIN_LIMITED`. It does not alter the already established existence of cosmological modal organization.

**Activation status:** CANDIDATE, NOT FROZEN.

## PREG-C candidate lane

### PREG-C-CAND-01 — Late GR structure-curvature feedback produces a jointly testable expansion/growth contribution

**Candidate claim text**

Within a preregistered late-time domain and covariant/gauge-robust averaging or multiscale construction, GR-native coupling among inhomogeneous expansion, shear, averaged curvature, and structure evolution produces a nonzero late-time contribution whose joint effect on expansion and growth is distinguishable from the strongest matched homogeneous-background comparator.

**Scope**

Late matter era / nonlinear structure. This is explicitly a partial-architecture claim and makes no early-CMB replacement claim.

**MFR-02 provenance**

DOMAIN_THEORY + EXTERNAL_THEORY + FRAMEWORK_DERIVED.

**MFR-03 target native objects**

At minimum:
- kinematical backreaction or the native equivalent;
- averaged curvature or a covariantly matched geometric quantity;
- expansion history;
- growth observable.

**MFR-04 representation / validity regime**

Freeze averaging domain, foliation/gauge, scale range, matter model, boundary conditions, and closure assumptions.

Refuse physical interpretation if the claimed contribution disappears or changes sign/order under the preregistered admissible gauge/slicing controls without a domain-native reason.

**MFR-05 strongest native comparator**

Matched homogeneous LambdaCDM/FLRW evolution with the same background parameters plus the strongest available standard nonlinear structure calculation that does not include the candidate averaged-feedback term as an independent explanatory object.

**MFR-06 competing explanations**

- ordinary parameter shifts in LambdaCDM;
- selection/volume effects;
- gauge/slicing artifact;
- conventional nonlinear corrections already captured by the comparator;
- effective-fluid bookkeeping with no independent predictive content.

**MFR-07 expected response**

The candidate architecture must make a joint, preregistered prediction for expansion and growth. Distance/expansion improvement without the corresponding growth behavior does not pass.

**MFR-08 decision rule candidate**

PASS candidate only if:
1. the feedback quantity is nonzero beyond frozen numerical/representation uncertainty;
2. the sign/order of the effect is prospectively correct for both expansion and growth;
3. the result survives gauge/slicing and domain-size controls;
4. the joint prediction improves or explains untouched evidence beyond the frozen comparator without post-result retuning.

**MFR-09 uncertainty / indeterminate zone**

Predefine numerical, cosmic-variance, domain-boundary, gauge, and closure uncertainty. If the candidate effect is comparable to the combined uncertainty envelope, return `INDETERMINATE`.

**MFR-10 independence / leakage map**

The literature used to discover the mechanism cannot count as decisive evidence. Closure selection, averaging scale, and target observable definitions must be frozen before opening the holdout.

**MFR-11 multiplicity**

Freeze domain sizes, redshift bins, observable family, closure family, and allowed summary statistics.

**MFR-12 decisive evidence route candidate**

A held-out relativistic simulation suite or independent survey/simulation comparison in which expansion- and growth-sensitive observables are evaluated jointly after all architecture definitions are frozen.

**MFR-13 explicit falsifier**

The preregistered feedback contribution is gauge/foliation unstable, numerically negligible in the target regime, has the wrong joint sign/order, or does not improve/explain the held-out expansion+growth response beyond the comparator.

**MFR-14 failure consequence**

`NO_ADDED_CONGLOMERATE_VALUE`, `DOMAIN_LIMITED`, or retirement of the tested closure/scale regime. Failure does not close other P0-D architecture routes.

**Activation status:** HIGH-PRIORITY CANDIDATE, NOT FROZEN.

### PREG-C-CAND-02 — Matter-boundary / edge architecture produces a testable late gravitational contribution

**Candidate claim text**

For a preregistered class of nonlinear bound structures, a covariantly defined matter-boundary/edge contribution generated within standard GR predicts a reproducible part of the observed gravitational response that is not present in the matched baryonic baseline and is not introduced as a new material component.

**Scope**

Galaxy or larger late nonlinear structures only. No early-Universe claim.

**MFR-02 provenance**

EXTERNAL_THEORY + FRAMEWORK_DERIVED.

**MFR-03 target native object**

The exact covariant edge/boundary term and its predicted contribution to a specified observable such as circular velocity, lensing, or another gravitational response.

**MFR-04 representation / validity regime**

Freeze the spacetime-surgery/boundary definition, matter-horizon construction, symmetry assumptions, structure class, and observable mapping.

**MFR-05 strongest native comparator**

Same baryonic mass model under ordinary GR without the candidate edge term, plus a standard halo-based fit as a phenomenological performance comparator.

**MFR-06 competing explanations**

- conventional dark halo;
- baryonic mass-model error;
- modified-gravity phenomenology;
- fitting flexibility in the boundary construction.

**MFR-07 expected response**

A parameter-constrained edge term predicts a held-out radial or structural gravitational response with correct sign/shape and transfers across more than one system without per-object free retuning that simply recreates a halo fit.

**MFR-08 decision rule candidate**

Promotion requires:
1. covariant definition fixed before holdout;
2. no object-by-object unconstrained tuning;
3. out-of-sample performance beyond the matched baryonic baseline;
4. competitive explanatory compression or predictive value relative to the frozen halo comparator;
5. lensing/dynamics consistency where both are available.

**MFR-09 uncertainty / indeterminate zone**

Include baryonic mass uncertainty, distance/inclination uncertainty, boundary-identification uncertainty, and numerical solution uncertainty.

**MFR-10 independence / leakage map**

Discovery galaxies/structures must be disjoint from decisive holdout objects. Any system used to motivate the functional form is excluded from P1 confirmation.

**MFR-11 multiplicity**

Freeze structure class, radial range, permitted nuisance parameters, observable family, and all boundary-identification alternatives.

**MFR-12 decisive evidence route candidate**

Independent galaxies or structures not used in the source derivation, ideally with both dynamical and lensing information.

**MFR-13 explicit falsifier**

The frozen edge construction cannot predict the held-out response without unconstrained retuning, fails lensing/dynamics consistency, or offers no added predictive value over the baryonic baseline and no competitive compression relative to the halo comparator.

**MFR-14 failure consequence**

`EDGE_ROUTE_NOT_PROMOTED` / `DOMAIN_LIMITED`. It does not falsify PREG-C-CAND-01.

**Activation status:** P0-Q QUALIFICATION NEEDED BEFORE FREEZE.

## PREG-J candidate lane

### PREG-J-CAND-01 — Late cosmological behavior is better represented by joint scalar-modal-conglomerate state than by any isolated layer

**Candidate claim text**

For a preregistered late-time cosmological transition or nonlinear-structure domain, the joint state composed of native scalar summaries, modal/subspace organization, and conglomerate coupling/feedback contains reproducible predictive information about the realized gravitational response that is not recoverable from any one layer alone under matched model complexity.

**Scope**

Late-time structure/feedback only. This is a joint-meaning claim, not a universal chi claim.

**MFR-02 provenance**

FRAMEWORK_DERIVED, with DOMAIN_THEORY objects only.

**MFR-03 target native objects**

A frozen triplet:
- scalar/local summary;
- modal/subspace object;
- conglomerate/system coupling object;
plus a predefined observable target.

**MFR-04 representation / validity regime**

Each layer retains native naming and its own validity conditions. The joint mapping must state exactly what information is preserved, transformed, or newly generated.

**MFR-05 strongest native comparator**

Matched single-layer and pairwise models with equalized complexity/capacity, plus the strongest standard domain model for the target observable.

**MFR-06 competing explanations**

Any apparent joint advantage is due to extra parameters, duplicated information, leakage, scale mixing, or generic nonlinear feature stacking.

**MFR-07 expected response**

The frozen joint representation produces a reproducible out-of-sample gain or a preregistered qualitative prediction that no isolated layer reproduces under matched complexity.

**MFR-08 decision rule candidate**

PASS candidate only if:
1. each component passes its own representation-validity checks;
2. joint performance exceeds all frozen single-layer and pairwise comparators by a preregistered margin or categorical criterion;
3. the gain survives complexity matching and leakage controls;
4. the result transfers to an untouched system/volume/epoch.

**MFR-09 uncertainty / indeterminate zone**

Set from resampling across systems/volumes, representation uncertainty, and model-complexity matching before holdout.

**MFR-10 independence / leakage map**

No statistic used to define the joint representation may be selected on the decisive holdout. Shared source variables across layers must be explicitly mapped.

**MFR-11 multiplicity**

Freeze all candidate scalar summaries, modes/subspaces, conglomerate variables, interaction terms, and target observables.

**MFR-12 decisive evidence route candidate**

Independent simulation volume or observational system family withheld from representation construction and comparator selection.

**MFR-13 explicit falsifier**

The joint representation does not outperform or uniquely explain the target beyond matched single/pairwise/domain-native comparators, or the gain disappears under leakage/complexity controls.

**MFR-14 failure consequence**

`NO_ADDED_JOINT_VALUE`. Scalar, modal, or conglomerate claims may survive independently.

**Activation status:** HIGH-PRIORITY CANDIDATE, NOT FROZEN.
