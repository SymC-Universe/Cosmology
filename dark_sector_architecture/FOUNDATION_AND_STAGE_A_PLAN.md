# Cosmic Stability Architecture: Dark Matter and Dark Energy as Emergent System Roles

**Status:** Stage A foundation synthesis / residual-question reconstruction  
**Branch:** `dark-sector-stability-architecture`  
**Scientific rule:** architecture first; no dark fluid, hidden medium, unified substance, new force, or new material component is introduced as a premise.

## Core Ontology Constraint

This project does **not** hypothesize that dark matter and dark energy are:
- one dark fluid;
- two phases of one substance;
- a hidden medium;
- a new material sector;
- a new force;
- a phenomenological substance with effective stiffness or viscosity.

The working hypothesis is system-level:

> The cosmological system may contain an inherited and feedback-mediated stability architecture whose observable functions overlap with phenomena conventionally assigned to dark matter and dark energy.

Inheritance, feedback, emergence, and any dark-sector interpretation are research targets, not premises.

## Research Question

> Can the phenomena conventionally assigned to dark matter and dark energy be recovered, in whole or in part, as observable functions of the Universe's native stability architecture without introducing a new substance or force?

The answer is not assumed to be yes.

## Competing Hypotheses

### H0: Standard LambdaCDM ontology is sufficient
Cold dark matter and Lambda remain distinct physical components. Architectural language adds no predictive or explanatory content beyond the standard model.

### H1: Architectural emergence
The native cosmological system contains organization, coupling, inheritance, and/or feedback whose system-level functions reproduce part or all of the gravitational phenomenology conventionally assigned to dark matter and dark energy.

### H2: Partial architectural emergence
Only part of the conventional dark phenomenology is architectural. One or more conventional components remain physically required.

### H3: Architectural failure
No gauge-robust architecture adds explanatory or predictive content beyond standard component-based cosmology.

# Stage A: Foundation Synthesis and Residual-Question Reconstruction

Stage A occurs **before new experimental, numerical, or observational confirmation work**.

Its purpose is to determine what the relevant literature already says when the pieces bearing directly on the frozen hypothesis are assembled without adding unearned SymC interpretation.

The project must not experimentally rediscover a result already established or already logically implied by the combined prior literature and then present it as a new finding.

## A0. Freeze the hypothesis and scope

Before targeted synthesis, preserve:
- the exact research question;
- the no-substance/no-new-force ontology constraint;
- the system boundary and cosmological regimes of interest;
- the candidate meanings of inheritance, feedback, emergence, scalar chi, and broader Chi without presuming they exist;
- the strongest standard nulls and comparators;
- and the claim ceiling at entry.

## A1. Extract only hypothesis-relevant components from each foundation paper

For every paper admitted to the foundation set, record:
- the exact result, equation, or empirical finding relevant to the hypothesis;
- the native model and assumptions under which it holds;
- whether the result is mathematical, model-conditional, observational, inferential, or speculative;
- the scale and regime in which it applies;
- what it establishes;
- what it does not establish;
- known objections or incompatible assumptions;
- and whether it can be combined with another paper without changing meaning.

No paper is treated as endorsing the SymC hypothesis merely because one of its results can be used in the synthesis.

## A2. Build the literature-native architecture before adding SymC interpretation

Assemble the extracted components into a compatibility and dependency map.

The synthesis must distinguish:
- relationships explicitly established in prior work;
- relationships that follow algebraically from established results;
- relationships that require an additional assumption;
- relationships that are incompatible across model classes;
- missing links;
- and genuine contradictions.

The first composite object is therefore not a SymC model. It is a **literature-native reconstruction of the current known world relevant to the hypothesis**.

## A3. Ask what the existing literature says together

Evaluate the frozen hypothesis against the assembled literature without new decisive testing.

For each material subclaim, assign one of:
- `ALREADY_ESTABLISHED`;
- `PARTIALLY_ESTABLISHED`;
- `SUPPORTED_ONLY_WITH_ADDITIONAL_ASSUMPTION`;
- `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`;
- `CONTRADICTED_IN_TESTED_REGIME`;
- `UNRESOLVED`;
- `INCOMPATIBLE_FOUNDATIONS`;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

The synthesis may itself show that the apparent new hypothesis is already known, is a novel integration of known pieces, requires a missing mathematical bridge, or fails before any new experiment is justified.

## A4. Apply Hunting Friction to the synthesis

Before any residual claim is preregistered:
- map shared-premise/common-mode risk across the selected literature;
- identify the strongest alternative interpretation;
- test whether an apparent connection is created only by wording, notation, gauge choice, averaging convention, or model mismatch;
- preserve negative and incompatible results;
- and record the bounded stopping reason.

Evidence determines the status of each proposed connection.

## A5. Define the residual question

Subtract from the original hypothesis everything that is:
- already established;
- already contradicted;
- dependent on an incompatible model;
- or only a restatement of existing theory.

What remains is the **residual scientific question**.

Only that residual is eligible for a new novelty or confirmation claim.

## A6. Preregister the residual confirmation

Before inspecting decisive new evidence, freeze:
- the residual claim;
- the native derivation or model needed to test it;
- the comparator/null;
- the carrier or mapping if inheritance is claimed;
- the closed-loop relation if feedback is claimed;
- the scalar/modal/system objects to be estimated;
- the chi/Chi joint-meaning question where licensed;
- the data or simulation inputs;
- the analysis and uncertainty rules;
- refusal conditions;
- falsifiers;
- and the outcome interpretation for positive, negative, partial, and null results.

Stage A ends only when the residual question and confirmation plan are frozen.

# Stage B: Confirmatory Native Reconstruction

Stage B begins only after Stage A produces a frozen residual claim.

Its purpose is to determine whether the residual relationship survives independent native derivation and new decisive evidence.

Candidate Stage B work may include:
1. gauge-robust native cosmological state construction;
2. first-order generator derivation;
3. Function and Limit Maps over k and a;
4. explicit inheritance-carrier tests;
5. explicit feedback-loop tests;
6. chi/Chi joint-meaning analysis where scalar chi is licensed;
7. observational projection onto growth, lensing, expansion, and other relevant observables;
8. comparison against LambdaCDM and strongest alternative native models.

A result already used to construct the Stage A synthesis cannot serve as untouched confirmation of the same residual claim.

# Critical Separations

A spectral transition is not automatically feedback.

Feedback is not automatically inheritance.

Inheritance is not automatically stability.

A literature-compatible narrative is not a derived mechanism.

A DM-like or DE-like observable role is not automatically evidence that a conventional component is absent.

A mathematical identity, a model result, and evidence about the actual Universe remain separate epistemic objects.

# Novelty Ceiling

Do not claim:
- first dark-sector unification;
- first eigenmode analysis;
- first stability analysis;
- first k-a spectral map;
- first expansion-growth test;
- disappearance of dark matter or dark energy;
- inheritance or feedback merely because the literature pieces can be arranged into a coherent story.

Novelty, if any, attaches only to the residual relationship, derivation, integration, prediction, or independent confirmation left after Stage A subtracts established knowledge.
