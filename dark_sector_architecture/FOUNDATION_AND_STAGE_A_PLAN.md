# Cosmic Stability Architecture: Dark Matter and Dark Energy as Emergent System Roles

**Status:** Stage A null-model reconstruction  
**Branch:** `dark-sector-stability-architecture`  
**Scientific rule:** architecture first; no dark fluid, hidden medium, unified substance, new force, or new material component is introduced as a premise.

## Core Ontology Constraint

This project does **not** hypothesize that dark matter and dark energy are:
- one dark fluid;
- two phases of one substance;
- a hidden medium;
- a new material sector;
- a new force;
- a phenomenological substance with effective stiffness or viscosity.

The working hypothesis is instead **system-level**:

> The cosmological system may develop and maintain an inherited stability architecture through dynamical feedback. The phenomena conventionally attributed to dark matter and dark energy may be observable functions of that architecture rather than evidence that two additional substances are the fundamental explanatory objects.

"Architecture" here means the organization of the native dynamical system: its degrees of freedom, constraints, couplings, feedback structure, modal organization, stability boundaries, and the way those structures persist or reorganize across cosmic evolution.

Inheritance and feedback are **research targets**, not assumed mechanisms. They must be derived or independently supported by the native cosmological equations and data.

## Research Question

> Can the phenomena conventionally assigned to dark matter and dark energy be recovered as complementary observable functions of the Universe's own inherited, feedback-mediated stability architecture, without positing a dark fluid, hidden medium, new force, or additional substance as the explanatory mechanism?

The answer is not assumed to be yes.

## Competing Hypotheses

### H0: Standard (Lambda)CDM Ontology Is Sufficient
Cold dark matter and (Lambda) remain distinct physical components. Any architectural description is only a re-expression of their standard dynamics.

### H1: Architectural Emergence
The native cosmological system contains a gauge-robust organization of modes, constraints, and feedback whose evolving functional roles reproduce the gravitational phenomenology conventionally assigned to dark matter and dark energy without requiring those roles themselves to be fundamental substances.

### H2: Partial Architectural Emergence
Only part of the dark phenomenology is architectural. One conventional dark component remains physically required while the other is better described as an emergent system-level function.

### H3: Architectural Failure
No gauge-robust dynamical architecture adds explanatory or predictive content beyond standard component-based cosmology.

## Architecture Targets

The project will test three distinct architectural properties.

### A. Inheritance
Does later cosmological organization retain measurable constraints, modal relationships, or stability structure inherited from earlier dynamical regimes?

Operationally, inheritance requires more than similarity. A candidate inherited feature must show:
1. a defined carrier or mapping across epochs;
2. persistence beyond trivial conservation identities;
3. specificity against alternative initial conditions or models;
4. a prospective consequence at a later epoch;
5. failure under a controlled counterfactual where the proposed carrier is altered.

### B. Feedback
Does the native cosmological system contain feedback capable of reorganizing or stabilizing its modal architecture?

Feedback must be identified from actual coupled equations or constraint structure. It cannot be inferred merely because one variable rises while another falls.

Candidate feedback must specify:
- state variable(s);
- sensing/dependence relation;
- response/coupling term;
- sign and timescale;
- closed-loop consequence;
- falsifier.

### C. Functional Emergence
Do observable roles normally labeled "dark matter" and "dark energy" emerge from the architecture?

The functional labels are:

**DM-like role**
- sustained gravitational clustering;
- structure support;
- lensing contribution;
- approximately pressureless behavior where observationally required.

**DE-like role**
- late-time accelerated expansion;
- suppression/reorganization of growth;
- required background pressure/effective stress behavior.

These roles are observational descriptors only. They are not assumed to identify substances.

## Critical Separation

A spectral transition is not automatically feedback.

Feedback is not automatically inheritance.

Inheritance is not automatically stability.

A DM-like or DE-like observable role is not automatically evidence that the corresponding conventional component is absent.

Each implication must be tested separately.

## Stage A Falsifiers

The architectural hypothesis loses support if:
- candidate spectral features vanish under gauge/basis-robust formulation;
- apparent feedback reduces to one-way dependence or bookkeeping identity;
- proposed inheritance reduces to trivial conservation or shared initial conditions;
- modal changes have no observable projection;
- the acceleration transition contains no independent architectural reorganization beyond standard (Lambda)CDM background evolution;
- standard component-based (Lambda)CDM explains the same observables with no additional predictive content from the architectural description.

All such failures are preserved as evidence.
