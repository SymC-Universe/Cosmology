## Development 001 - Early-Universe Dark-Matter Constraint

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**GitHub checkpoint before entry:** `99b3639f6776deb7b347112bbcb9b7dcc1192830`

### Scientific development

The admitted GR averaging, macroscopic-gravity, and perturbative-backreaction literature does not presently provide a quantitatively sufficient pre-recombination gravitational contribution that behaves like the cold-dark-matter role required by the CMB.

### Triggering evidence

- Li & Schwarz: perturbative kinematical backreaction becomes relatively less important toward early times.
- Brown, Robbers & Behrend: radiation-era backreaction is approximately radiation-like, not pressureless-matter-like.
- Paranjape: covariant macroscopic-gravity correlation terms remain tiny during radiation domination.
- Coley, Pelavas & Zalaletdinov: the macroscopic-gravity correlation solution is curvature-like, scaling as (a^{-2}), not CDM-like.
- Planck: the baryon budget is independently constrained while acoustic structure and lensing require stronger gravitational potentials than baryons alone provide under standard cosmology.

### Consequence

The currently conglomerated late-time structure/backreaction architecture cannot be extrapolated backward and treated as a complete replacement for the pre-recombination DM-like gravitational role.

Two branches remain open:

1. **Partial architecture:** the architecture explains or reorganizes only some later DM-like and/or DE-like phenomena.
2. **Primordial/inherited architecture:** a distinct architecture is already active before recombination and is carried forward through later cosmic transitions.

### Claim ceiling after development

Supported:
- multiscale/two-way GR architectures exist;
- shear and expansion variance can play opposing functional roles in averaged GR;
- late nonlinear structure can affect averaged curvature/expansion;
- formal memory/history dependence can arise under coarse graining.

Not supported:
- complete dark-matter replacement;
- pre-recombination CDM-like function from known late backreaction;
- one demonstrated architecture spanning CMB through late acceleration;
- SymC inheritance as the carrier connecting the eras.

### Exact restart point

Run a bounded Stage A conglomeration specifically for a **primordial/inherited carrier**. Search inflationary/primordial perturbation structure, radiation-era mode organization, conserved quantities and transfer maps across horizon entry/equality/recombination, geometrical memory, Weyl curvature, constraints, and any GR-native object capable of retaining or transmitting stability information into later structure.

A valid candidate must:
- exist before recombination;
- have a defined mathematical carrier;
- survive or transform through radiation-to-matter transition;
- influence later gravitational organization;
- not merely rename a dark substance;
- face CMB/BBN constraints directly;
- and connect to the late-time architecture without post hoc retuning.

## Development 002 - Primordial Carrier Found, Source Sufficiency Not Found

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**GitHub checkpoint before entry:** `02b037fcda07cba8573f3fbb475c67229b32885f`

### Scientific development

The primordial Stage A pass identifies a strong, standard-GR **inheritance carrier**: the gauge-invariant curvature perturbation, commonly represented by (zeta) on uniform-density hypersurfaces or the comoving curvature perturbation (mathcal R).

For adiabatic perturbations on sufficiently large scales, this quantity is conserved under well-defined conditions. It therefore carries primordial spatial organization across changes in the cosmic background and supplies the initial information from which later transfer functions and component perturbations evolve.

This resolves an important part of the inheritance question: cosmology already contains a mathematically explicit mechanism for carrying organized initial information forward across epochs.

However, that carrier is not itself an independent pressureless gravitational source.

### Triggering evidence

- Wands, Malik, Lyth & Liddle: on sufficiently large scales, the curvature perturbation on uniform-density hypersurfaces is conserved when non-adiabatic pressure is negligible.
- Lyth, Malik & Sasaki: the conservation result extends nonlinearly when pressure is a unique function of energy density.
- Weinberg: an adiabatic mode with nonzero constant (mathcal R) exists in the large-wavelength limit across all eras under mild assumptions.
- Standard CMB perturbation theory: after horizon entry, the photon-baryon fluid undergoes acoustic oscillation and diffusion, while in standard cosmology pressureless CDM supplies persistent gravitational potential wells. Hu & Dodelson emphasize that the observed high third acoustic peak is strong evidence for a pressureless component dominating the gravitational potential at recombination.
- Malik & Wands: scalar matter and metric perturbations are linked by the Einstein constraint equations. In standard GR the scalar metric potentials are therefore not a freely specifiable independent gravitational reservoir once the matter/radiation state is fixed.

### Consequence

The full architecture hypothesis is sharpened rather than closed.

The **inheritance carrier is no longer the principal missing object**. Standard cosmology already supplies one.

The unresolved problem is now **source sufficiency**:

> Can native GR organization, correlations, nonlinear geometry, or another non-substance architectural degree of freedom convert the inherited primordial curvature information into the non-oscillating pre-recombination gravitational response demanded by the CMB, without inserting a pressureless dark component or new force?

If the answer is no, the no-dark-substance hypothesis cannot cover the full DM-like role and must narrow to a partial architecture.

### Claim ceiling after development

Supported:
- primordial organization has a well-defined conserved carrier in the adiabatic large-scale regime;
- later cosmological states inherit information from this primordial curvature field through transfer dynamics;
- this inheritance is mathematically stronger and more direct than invoking a generic phenomenological memory kernel.

Not supported:
- (zeta) or (mathcal R) as an independent dark-matter-like source;
- scalar geometry alone maintaining the required subhorizon pre-recombination potential in standard linear GR;
- a complete no-dark-substance explanation of the CMB gravitational demand;
- an identified physical bridge from the primordial carrier to the late (Q_{mathcal D})-curvature architecture.

### Exact restart point

Test the **source-sufficiency residual** before narrowing the hypothesis.

The next bounded Stage A pass should examine whether standard GR supplies any non-substance scalar gravitational contribution beyond linear component dynamics through:
- nonlinear scalar mode coupling;
- second-order metric/constraint terms;
- Weyl/tidal organization;
- covariant correlation tensors;
- primordial geometric correlations;
- genuinely nonlocal or coarse-grained gravitational terms already active before recombination.

The pass must explicitly distinguish:
1. an independent physical degree of freedom;
2. a constrained metric response;
3. an averaging/correlation term;
4. an effective rewriting of matter dynamics;
5. a true additional source.

If all viable candidates remain constrained, tiny, radiation-like, curvature-like, or observationally insufficient, Route I closes and the investigation narrows to the partial architecture.

## Development 003 - Inheritance Extends Across Scalar, Modal, and Conglomerate Layers

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**GitHub checkpoint before entry:** `7d79d66494fa630d1dba7a6b34301a8c46b93797`

### Scientific development

The scalar-only interpretation of Development 002 was incomplete.

The literature shows that primordial inheritance can be represented at all three currently admitted SymC starting layers:

1. **Scalar:** gauge-invariant curvature variables such as (zeta) and (mathcal R) carry compressed adiabatic large-scale information.
2. **Vector/Modal:** the primordial perturbation state has a regular mode basis containing adiabatic and isocurvature directions. These modes are propagated through the Einstein-Boltzmann system by scale- and time-dependent transfer dynamics.
3. **Conglomerate/System:** the realized primordial state is described not only by individual mode amplitudes but by their auto- and cross-correlations. The matrix-valued primordial power spectrum is transported into later observables through the mode transfer functions.

Therefore cosmological inheritance is not adequately represented by one conserved scalar.

### Triggering evidence

- Bucher, Moodley & Turok derive the regular primordial scalar-mode basis: adiabatic, baryon-isocurvature, CDM-isocurvature, neutrino-density-isocurvature, and neutrino-velocity-isocurvature modes.
- The most general Gaussian primordial scalar state is represented by a symmetric matrix-valued power spectrum (P_{ij}(k)) containing modal auto- and cross-correlations.
- CMB observables are obtained by applying mode-specific transfer functions to the primordial mode organization. Schematically,

  where the linear state representation and conventions make this expression applicable.
- Planck polarization strongly constrains the observed primordial organization toward the adiabatic subspace, with percent-level upper limits on several non-adiabatic contributions.
- Nadkarni-Ghosh & Refregier show that the Einstein-Boltzmann generator itself changes eigenstructure over the (k-a) plane, so the inherited state is dynamically reorganized rather than carried as a single fixed scalar.
- A direct primordial-medium eigenmode analysis identifies a strongly acoustic baryon-radiation mode and a distinct gravitational-collapse mode dominated by the pressureless component in the standard matter content.

### Reframing of the pre-recombination residual

The early dark-matter problem is more accurately a **modal-function problem**.

The standard primordial system contains:
- oscillatory/acoustic photon-baryon modes;
- free-streaming neutrino response;
- and, when a pressureless CDM component is present, a comparatively non-acoustic gravitational-growth/collapse mode.

The residual is therefore not merely whether a scalar metric potential can be maintained.

It is:

> Can the native GR architecture realize the required persistent non-acoustic gravitational mode through modal or conglomerate organization without tying that function to a material cold-dark-matter degree of freedom?

### Claim ceiling after development

Supported:
- primordial inheritance has a scalar representation;
- primordial inheritance has a modal representation;
- primordial inheritance has a conglomerate/correlation representation;
- linear transfer dynamics supply an explicit transformation law connecting early modal organization to later observables;
- the observed primordial conglomerate is strongly adiabatic-dominated;
- modal reorganization across (k) and (a) is established.

Not supported:
- that the pressureless gravitational mode is realizable without a material CDM degree of freedom;
- that scalar, modal, or conglomerate inheritance alone replaces the dark-matter function;
- that the three current starting representations exhaust cosmological (Chi);
- that any primordial scalar or modal object qualifies as lower-case (chi) without a licensed stability-coordinate derivation.

### Exact restart point

Continue Stage A with a **modal/conglomerate source-sufficiency pass**, not a scalar-only pass.

Test whether a persistent non-acoustic gravitational branch can arise from:
- nonlinear scalar-mode coupling;
- Weyl/shear/tidal organization;
- covariant macroscopic-gravity correlation modes;
- non-Markovian/coarse-grained collective modes;
- scalar-vector-tensor coupling beyond linear order;
- constraint-induced collective modes;
- or an additional architecture component not represented by the current scalar/modal/conglomerate starting basis.

For each candidate, preserve:
- native degrees of freedom;
- modal spectrum/subspace;
- participation structure;
- relation to scalar observables;
- conglomerate coupling;
- transfer across equality/recombination;
- observational signature;
- and whether the candidate is a physical mode, constrained response, effective rewriting, or genuinely new system function.
